# Dataset card and source log
* **Team, primary language, variety, version and date:** Team 4 (SautiByte), Kiswahili (sw-KE), Kenyan conversational/code-switched variety. v1.0, 10 November 2026.
* **Task and intended use:** Intent classification (10 MASSIVE labels). Intended to train and evaluate a request-routing assistant in natural Kenyan Swahili.
* **Data fields, counts and splits:** 250 total records. 150 `train`, 50 `dev`, 50 `test` (Exactly 15/5/5 split for each of the 10 intent labels).
* **Sources: dataset title, version, URL, source IDs, exact licence, permission reference, transformations:** Original team-authored text (`source_dataset: team_authored`, `source_method: original_human`). 
* **Contributor permission for newly authored text (private evidence held separately):** Documented internal team consent (`licence_or_permission: documented_contributor_permission`).
* **Annotation and language-review process:** Text drafted by one member, labeled, and rotated to a different fluent team member for independent review of naturalness and label accuracy.
* **Known limitations and excluded uses:** Dataset focuses strictly on 10 predefined intents. Small vocabulary size (250 records) means it is a prototype and not a general-purpose conversational AI benchmark.
